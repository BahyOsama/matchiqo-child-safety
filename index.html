Child Safety & Protection

Safety is a priority.
Protection is our commitment.

At Matchiqo, we are committed to protecting people from abuse, exploitation, and harmful behavior. We maintain a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation and are committed to promoting a safer digital environment.

Effective November 23, 2026
Official Safety Standards

Zero Tolerance for CSAE

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) is strictly prohibited. Matchiqo does not tolerate the abuse, exploitation, sexualization, grooming, or endangerment of minors in any form. Any violation of these standards may result in immediate enforcement action, including permanent account termination.

Our Standards

Clear rules. Serious protection.

These standards apply to all users, accounts, interactions, communications, and content associated with Matchiqo. Violations are not tolerated.

Prohibition of CSAE

Matchiqo strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including any activity that facilitates, promotes, encourages, or enables harm to minors.

  • Sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
  • Sexualized content involving children.
  • Attempts to facilitate abuse or exploitative activities.

Age Restrictions

Matchiqo is designed for adults. Anyone under the age of 18 is prohibited from accessing or using the platform.

  • Users must be at least 18 years old.
  • Underage accounts may be restricted or removed.
  • Attempts to bypass age restrictions are prohibited.

Grooming & Predatory Behavior

Any attempt to manipulate, approach, groom, solicit, threaten, or exploit a minor is strictly forbidden.

  • Inappropriate contact or solicitation involving minors.
  • Manipulation, coercion, or grooming behavior.
  • Attempts to move exploitative interactions elsewhere.

CSAM Prohibition

Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) is strictly prohibited, including creating, uploading, sharing, distributing, requesting, or promoting such material.

  • No creation or distribution of CSAM.
  • No requests for child sexual abuse material.
  • No attempts to use Matchiqo to facilitate CSAM.

Reporting & Enforcement

Child safety concerns and policy violations may be reported through available reporting channels. Reports are taken seriously and may lead to appropriate enforcement measures.

  • Review of reported safety concerns.
  • Removal of violating content where applicable.
  • Account restrictions, suspension, or permanent bans.

Legal Compliance & Cooperation

Matchiqo is committed to complying with applicable child protection and online safety laws and responding appropriately to lawful requests from relevant authorities.

  • Compliance with applicable child safety laws.
  • Appropriate cooperation with competent authorities.
  • Handling safety matters in accordance with applicable law.
